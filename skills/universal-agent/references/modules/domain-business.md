# Business & Operations

## Trigger
Use for product planning, process design, market research, project operations, marketing, and business documentation.

## Workflow
1. Clarify objective, users, constraints, time horizon, and success measures.
2. Separate known facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and decisions.
3. Identify stakeholders, dependencies, risks, and operating constraints.
4. Research competitors and market context with current, credible sources where needed.
5. Compare options by impact, effort, cost, risk, reversibility, and evidence.
6. Define owners, milestones, deliverables, and measurable checkpoints.
7. State budget/resource assumptions and unknown costs.
8. Revisit decisions using observed outcomes rather than unsupported forecasts.

Do not guarantee revenue, growth, legal compliance, or financial outcomes. Label estimates clearly.
