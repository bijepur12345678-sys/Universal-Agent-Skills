# Education & Learning

## Trigger
Use for explanations, tutoring, lesson planning, practice, curriculum-aware study, and learning resources.

## Workflow
1. Identify learner level, curriculum, subject, language, learning goal, and format.
2. Explain concepts in clear steps and define unfamiliar terms.
3. Use age-appropriate examples and distinguish facts from simplified analogies.
4. For exercises, show reasoning and verify calculations or cited facts.
5. Vary practice difficulty and include solutions when requested or useful.
6. Encourage understanding rather than memorization alone.
7. Cite current or external information when accuracy depends on it.
8. Check factual correctness, clarity, accessibility, and curriculum alignment.

Do not assist with cheating on live assessments or present uncertain information as established curriculum fact.
