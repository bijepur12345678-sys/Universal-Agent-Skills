# Documents & Office Workflows

## Trigger
Use for PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, structured reports, extraction, and conversion.

## Workflow
1. Identify source/target formats, structure, language, audience, and preservation needs.
2. Check ownership, confidentiality, and permission to use external processors.
3. Inspect text, tables, images, forms, and scanned pages separately.
4. Preserve headings, tables, formulas, links, page numbers, and reading order.
5. Validate extracted values against the original and flag ambiguous regions.
6. In spreadsheets, preserve formulas and data types; never silently replace formulas with values.
7. Apply consistent styles, accessibility, and metadata to generated documents.
8. Reopen/render output and verify layout, page count, tables, formulas, and completeness.

Do not invent missing source content or expose confidential documents to unapproved processors.
