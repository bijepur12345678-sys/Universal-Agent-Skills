# Data Science & Analytics

## Trigger
Use for data cleaning, statistics, visualization, ETL, dashboards, forecasting, and experimental analysis.

## Workflow
1. Define question, population, metrics, time window, and decision.
2. Inspect schema, provenance, missingness, duplicates, units, time zones, and sampling bias.
3. Preserve raw data and create reproducible transformations.
4. Choose methods appropriate to data and test assumptions.
5. Separate exploration from confirmatory analysis.
6. Prevent train/test leakage and use suitable predictive baselines.
7. Show uncertainty, intervals, limitations, and alternative explanations.
8. Label axes, units, denominators, filters, and aggregation rules.
9. Reconcile summaries with source totals and provide reproducible results.

Do not infer causation from correlation without supporting design or fabricate observations.
