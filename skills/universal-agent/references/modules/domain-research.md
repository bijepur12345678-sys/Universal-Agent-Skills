# Research & Evidence Synthesis

## Trigger
Use for deep research, technology evaluation, source discovery, literature review, and fact-checking.

## Workflow
1. Split the task into answerable questions and define inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Prefer primary sources: official docs, standards, original papers, release notes, and source repositories.
3. Record URL, publication/update date, event date where relevant, author, and evidence strength.
4. Open material sources and verify underlying content rather than relying on snippets.
5. Deduplicate sources and separate fact, inference, opinion, and unknown.
6. Check counterevidence, version drift, conflicts, and commercial incentives.
7. Compare capability, compatibility, license, maintenance, cost, security, and limitations.
8. Cite material external claims.
9. State what was searched and what remains unverified.

Never invent sources, statistics, dates, or product capabilities.
