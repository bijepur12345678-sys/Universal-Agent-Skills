# Internal Module: License and Provenance Review

## Trigger
Use before copying, adapting, redistributing, or publishing third-party code, Skills, scripts, data, assets, or documentation.

## Procedure
1. Identify exact source repository, file path, author/contributor, immutable commit or release, and retrieval date.
2. Read the root license and any nested LICENSE, NOTICE, headers, or per-file terms.
3. Determine the applicable license at exact content scope. If uncertain, mark `review_required` and do not redistribute.
4. Check third-party contributions, trademarks, models, datasets, and external assets.
5. Record required notices, attribution, license text, modification notices, patent terms, and source-disclosure requirements.
6. Choose reference-only, independent reimplementation, verified redistribution, or reject.
7. Preserve immutable source revision and provenance.

A public repository is not public domain. Never convert an unknown license into MIT or Apache-2.0. This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.
