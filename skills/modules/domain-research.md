# Domain Module: Research & Evidence Synthesis

## Trigger
Use for deep research, technology evaluation, source discovery, comparison, literature review, or fact-checking.

## Workflow
1. Turn the request into answerable sub-questions and define inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Search diverse angles and prioritize primary sources, official documentation, source repositories, standards, and original research.
3. Record source URL, publication/update date, event date where relevant, author, and evidence strength.
4. Open important sources and verify claims against the underlying document rather than relying on snippets.
5. Deduplicate same-source results and separate facts, inference, opinion, and unknowns.
6. Check counterevidence, version drift, conflicts, and commercial incentives.
7. Use a concise comparison schema for candidates: capability, compatibility, license, maintenance, cost, security, limitations, and fit.
8. Cite every material externally sourced claim.
9. State what was searched and what could not be verified.

## Outputs
A source-grounded synthesis, comparison matrix, evidence log, and explicit recommendation with uncertainty.

## Boundaries
Do not invent citations, publication dates, statistics, or product capabilities. Absence from search results is not proof of absence.
